Card Games for iMessage

Your seat at the table is ready.

Three classic card games that live inside your text threads. Start a game in a one-on-one conversation or a group chat, and everyone plays whenever they get to it.

Download on the App Store

Card Games for iMessage is a standalone iMessage app. There is no separate app to open, no account to create, and no lobby to wait in — a game is just a turn in the conversation, played at whatever pace suits the people in it.

The games

Gin Rummy

Standard straight Gin when playing with seven cards or in group chat games, with full deadwood calculation in one-on-one ten-card games.

Crazy 8s

8s are wild, 2s make your opponent draw two, Queens skip, and Aces reverse the direction of play.

Golf

Aim for the lowest score with a six-card grid.

What it costs

All three games are free, and there are no advertisements. The only in-app purchases are optional card backs — cosmetic deck designs bought once through Apple's in-app purchase system. A card back bought here also unlocks in Poker for iMessage, and vice versa.

Accessibility and languages

The app is fully localized in 18 languages, and is built against Apple's accessibility standards: complete VoiceOver and Voice Control support for addressing individual cards and game elements, Dynamic Type support throughout, and reduced animation when Reduce Motion is enabled.

Privacy

The app contains no analytics, no advertising frameworks, and no networking code. Nothing about you or your games is sent to us, because there is no server to send it to. See the privacy policy for the full detail.
