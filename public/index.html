Card games that live in your text threads.

Owl Orchard is a small independent software studio in Oregon. We build standalone iMessage apps that let you play real card games with the people you are already texting — no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and nothing to switch away from.

Our apps are built entirely with SwiftUI and run inside the Messages app itself. A game is a turn in a conversation: you play a hand, it lands in the thread, and the other player picks it up whenever they get to it. It works in one-on-one threads and in group chats, and it works across time zones, because nobody has to be online at the same moment.

Every game in every app is free. There are no advertisements and no subscriptions. The only thing we sell is optional card backs — cosmetic deck designs, purchased once, through Apple's own in-app purchase system.

How we build

Nothing leaves your device. Our apps contain no analytics, no advertising frameworks, no third-party SDKs, and no networking code of any kind. There is no Owl Orchard server, because there is nothing for it to store. Game state travels through Apple's Messages framework in your own conversation, exactly like a photo or a link would.

Everyone should be able to play. Both apps ship fully localized in 18 languages and are built to Apple's accessibility standards — complete VoiceOver and Voice Control support down to individual cards, Dynamic Type, and honored Reduce Motion settings.

Buy it once. Card backs are a one-time purchase, and a design bought in one of our apps unlocks in the other automatically.
