Poker for iMessage

Your seat at the table is ready.

Poker for iMessage brings three classic poker variants into the conversations you are already having. Deal a hand in a group chat, and everyone plays at their own pace.

It is a standalone iMessage app — there is no separate app to open, no account to create, and no lobby to wait in. Hands are played turn by turn, so a game can run over an afternoon or over a week without anyone needing to be online at the same time.

The games

Texas Hold'Em

Each player gets two hole cards, and five community cards are shared across the flop, turn, and river betting rounds.

Omaha

Each player gets four hole cards and must use exactly two of them, plus three community cards, to make their best five-card hand.

7 Card Stud

No community cards. Players get a mix of face-up and face-down cards across multiple betting rounds, with a bring-in and antes instead of blinds.

What it costs

All three games are free, and there are no advertisements. The only in-app purchases are optional card backs — cosmetic deck designs bought once through Apple's in-app purchase system. A card back bought here also unlocks in Card Games for iMessage, and vice versa.

No real money

Poker for iMessage is a simulated card game. Chips have no monetary value, they cannot be purchased, and they cannot be cashed out, transferred, or exchanged for anything of value. There is no wagering of real money or real-world items in the app, and the app is rated for mature audiences accordingly.

Accessibility and languages

The app is fully localized in 18 languages, and is built against Apple's accessibility standards: complete VoiceOver and Voice Control support for addressing individual cards and game elements, Dynamic Type support throughout, and reduced animation when Reduce Motion is enabled.

Privacy

The app contains no analytics, no advertising frameworks, and no networking code. Nothing about you or your games is sent to us, because there is no server to send it to. See the privacy policy for the full detail.
