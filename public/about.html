About

Homegrown in Oregon.

Owl Orchard builds native applications for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Small on purpose

Owl Orchard is independent, with no investors and no growth target. Nobody here making the case for an analytics SDK, nobody who needs a retention metric to move, and no reason for any of these apps to want anything from you beyond the time you choose to spend in them. The two iMessage apps ship with no networking code whatsoever. The other three are specific, in writing, about the few things that leave your device.

Where it is

The studio is registered and run in Oregon, United States. Support email is answered from here, usually within a day or two.
