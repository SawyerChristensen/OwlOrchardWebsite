About

An independent studio in Oregon.

Owl Orchard LLC builds native applications for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Five of them so far, and every one is free.

The catalogue has nothing obvious in common. One app is a chess variant from 1936. One is a live feed of congressional roll-call votes. One turns whatever your Mac is playing into Milkdrop visuals. Two of them live inside iMessage and never open a window of their own.

What they share is how they are built. Every app is written in Swift against Apple’s own frameworks — no web views in a native wrapper, no cross-platform runtime between the app and the hardware. Every app is free to download and free to use. Every app is localized well past English, and built to work with VoiceOver, Voice Control, Dynamic Type, and Reduce Motion rather than around them.

Small on purpose

Owl Orchard is independent, with no investors and no growth target. That is a real constraint: releases are slower than a funded studio’s, and there is no overnight support rotation.

It buys something back, though. There is nobody here making the case for an analytics SDK, nobody who needs a retention metric to move, and no reason for any of these apps to want anything from you beyond the time you choose to spend in them. The two iMessage apps ship with no networking code whatsoever. The other three are specific, in writing, about the few things that leave your device.

Where it is

The studio is registered and run in Oregon, United States. Support email is answered from here, usually within a day or two.
