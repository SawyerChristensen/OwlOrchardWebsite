Privacy Policy

Card Games for iMessage — last updated August 31, 2026

Card Games for iMessage is published by Owl Orchard LLC, an Oregon limited liability company. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information. The short version: the app collects nothing, and there is no Owl Orchard server for anything to be collected to.

Information we collect

None. Card Games for iMessage contains no analytics frameworks, no advertising frameworks, no crash-reporting services, no third-party software development kits, and no networking code of any kind. The app does not transmit information to Owl Orchard LLC or to any third party, because it has no capability to do so.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics. We do not track you across apps or websites, and the app does not ask for permission to do so because it never attempts it.

Information stored on your device

The app stores a small amount of data locally so it can remember your settings and what you own:

  • Game state and preferences — the games in progress in your conversations, and settings such as your selected card back.
  • Card back ownership — a list of product identifiers for the card backs you have purchased, for example Theme.Koi. This is stored in a shared app group container so that Card Games for iMessage and Card Games for iMessage can both recognize a purchase made in either one.

This data stays on your device. It is not transmitted to us and we cannot read it.

iCloud

So that a card back you bought on one device is available on your other devices, the app writes the same list of purchased card back identifiers to Apple's iCloud key-value storage. This contains product identifiers only — no personal information, no game content, and no message content.

That data lives in your own iCloud account, governed by Apple's privacy policy. Owl Orchard LLC has no access to it. You can disable this by turning off iCloud Drive for the app in your device settings; card backs will then remain available on the device where you bought them, and you can use Restore Purchases on other devices.

Your conversations

Card Games for iMessage is an iMessage app. Game moves travel as messages inside your own Messages conversation, handled entirely by Apple's Messages framework and subject to Apple's encryption and privacy practices. Those messages go to the people in your conversation. They never pass through any Owl Orchard system, and we have no ability to read, store, or recover them.

The app does not access your contacts or your message history. It can see only the conversation it has been opened in, and only the game data within it.

Purchases

Card backs are optional one-time in-app purchases processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see or handle your payment method, billing address, or Apple Account details. Apple provides us only with aggregate, anonymized sales reporting — unit counts and totals by territory — which cannot identify any individual purchaser.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Children

Card Games for iMessage does not knowingly collect information from children, and in fact collects no information from anyone. The app contains no advertising, no external links other than to Apple's own purchase and support pages, and no user-generated content beyond the card moves players exchange in their own conversations.

Your rights

Privacy laws including the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete, and port the personal information a company holds about you, and to opt out of its sale. We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, delete, or port. We have never sold or shared personal information, and we do not.

To remove everything the app has stored on your device, delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
