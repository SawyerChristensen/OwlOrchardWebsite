Support

Email support@owlorchard.com and a real person will answer. We are a one-person studio, so give us a day or two.

If you are reporting a bug, it helps enormously to include which app and game you were playing, your iOS version, and whether the game was a one-on-one thread or a group chat.

Frequently asked questions

I bought a card back in one app. Why don't I see it in the other?

You should — card backs are shared between Poker for iMessage and Card Games for iMessage. If a design you bought hasn't appeared in the other app yet, try these in order:

  • Open the other app and let it finish loading its card back list once. Purchases made on the same device usually appear immediately.
  • Make sure both devices are signed into the same iCloud account, and that iCloud Drive is enabled in Settings. Purchases sync between your devices through iCloud, which can take a minute or two after a purchase.
  • Use Restore Purchases in the app's card back menu.

If it still hasn't shown up, email us with the name of the card back and the app you bought it in, and we'll sort it out.

I have a new iPhone. Do I have to buy my card backs again?

No. Sign into the same Apple Account you bought them with and use Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, not your device.

Do I need to install the app for a friend to play with me?

Both players need the app installed to take turns. If someone in the thread doesn't have it, the message they receive includes a link to install it.

Is there real money in Poker for iMessage?

No. Chips have no monetary value. They cannot be bought, cashed out, transferred, or exchanged for anything of value. It is a simulated card game with no wagering.

Does the app work in a group chat?

Yes. Both apps support group games as well as one-on-one threads.

How do I request a refund?

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple, so refunds go through Apple rather than through us. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account, and select the purchase.

Do you collect any of my data?

No. Our apps contain no analytics, no advertising frameworks, and no networking code of any kind. See the privacy policy for Poker for iMessage or Card Games for iMessage.

Can I get the app in my language?

Both apps are fully localized in 18 languages and follow your device language automatically. If a translation reads badly to a native speaker, please tell us — that is genuinely useful feedback and we will fix it.
