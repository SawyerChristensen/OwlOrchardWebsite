Prism — Music Visualizer

Your music, rendered.

On the App StoremacOS 26+FreeApple Silicon
Download on the App Store Support

Scroll sideways for the rest →

Point Prism at any audio playing on your Mac — Spotify, Apple Music, a browser tab, anything — and it renders classic Milkdrop-style visuals in real time. Every colour, ripple, and kaleidoscoping shape reacts as the music happens.

Prism runs genuine .milk preset files through a real, vendored build of the projectM engine: the same format that powered two decades of Winamp visuals, not a lookalike. Drop in your own preset packs, browse the curated built-in library of more than 2,500, or drag a single .milk file onto the window.

  • Whatever is playing

    System audio is captured through Core Audio process taps, so Prism visualizes any app on the Mac rather than only the one music player it knows about.

  • 2,500+ real presets

    A curated, frame-rate-benchmarked library ships in the app, and any folder of .milk files can be pointed at instead. NestDrop favourites lists are read if your pack has one.

  • Album art that moves

    When Spotify or Music is playing, Prism lifts the artwork’s subject, pulls its colour palette, and composites it over the visuals with beat-synced parallax. Press ⌘A to hide it and watch the preset alone.

  • Smart preset matching

    Prism reads a track’s energy, mood, and tempo and picks a preset that suits it, instead of cycling at random.

  • Remembers where you were

    It reopens on the preset you left, keeps a session history you can step back through with the arrow keys, and keeps the previous session’s log around too.

  • Three ways to run it

    Prism.app full screen or windowed; a plug-in that appears inside Music’s own Visualizer menu; and a matching screen saver for when the Mac goes idle.

Built for the hardware

Prism is native to Apple Silicon. The projectM C++ engine is bridged into Metal through an ANGLE/EGL context, which keeps the original OpenGL preset semantics intact while rendering on Apple’s modern graphics stack. On a current Mac it clears 800 frames per second.

Privacy

Prism keeps no listening history and records nothing it captures. The one thing that leaves your Mac is the current track’s title and artist, sent to a free public music-data API so preset matching knows the song’s tempo and mood — with no account, device, or personal identifier attached.
